# 📊 Reebok Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze Reebok’s sales performance across multiple regions, product categories, and time periods.
The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights, enabling data-driven strategic decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Evaluate overall revenue and profit performance  
- Identify top-performing regions and product categories  
- Analyze monthly and quarterly sales trends  
- Measure category-wise and regional contribution to total sales  

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- A limited number of regions contribute the highest share of total revenue  
- Premium product categories generate higher profit margins  
- Seasonal sales spikes are observed during specific quarters  
- Emerging markets show strong growth potential  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Techniques Used
- Microsoft Excel  
- Pivot Tables  
- Pivot Charts  
- KPI Metrics (Revenue, Profit, Growth %)  
- Data Cleaning & Transformation  
- Interactive Filters & Slicers
